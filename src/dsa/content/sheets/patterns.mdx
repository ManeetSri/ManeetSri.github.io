Most problems announce their technique, if you know what to listen for. Read the constraints first ([what n allows](/dsa/sheets/big-o)), then scan for these signals. When two rows match, try the simpler technique first. The full treatment is in [Reading the pattern from constraints and wording](/dsa/learn/pat-recognition).

## When you're stuck

1. **Solve it by hand** on a small example and write down what you did. That is the algorithm.
2. **Write the brute force.** Then ask which work it repeats, and which structure could remember it.
3. **Sort it** and see what becomes easy.
4. **Ask what a hash map would remember.**
5. **Flip the question.** Count the complement, process from the right, or binary search the answer.
6. **Shrink it.** Solve it for n − 1, then ask how to extend that answer: recursion and DP.
